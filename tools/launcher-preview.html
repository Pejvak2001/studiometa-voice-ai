Launcher preview

Front-end only. No licence, no backend — the ring, the card and the motion are all client-side, so this shows exactly what a visitor sees. Pressing Start Call will fail to connect, which is expected here.

Replay

The real widget rings once at 4s, retracts at 17s, tries once more at ~62s, then stays quiet. A dismissal silences it for the whole session.

Sound

On by default now. Browsers block audio until you have clicked somewhere on the page, so click anywhere first, then Ring again, and you will hear the two-note chime. A site can still switch it off with teaseSound: false.

Position & language

Brand colour

Every accent in the card derives from this one value, because a plugin cannot assume anything about the host site's palette.

Reduced motion

Set your OS to “reduce motion” and reload: the tilt and waves stop, and the card simply fades in.
